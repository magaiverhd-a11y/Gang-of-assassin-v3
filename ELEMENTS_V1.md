# Elements v1

Twelve elements. Hits check weakness and resistance, then leave a status.

## Damage

- Weak: the hit deals **1.5×** damage and the number says **WEAK**.
- Resist: the hit deals **0.65×** damage (at least 1) and the number says **RESIST**.
- A weakness beats a resistance when more than one element is on the hit.
- This works both ways: your element against the foe, and the foe’s element against you.
- Each foe has an element from its kind and the raid.

## Status on a hit

| Element | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Fire | Burn. 1 damage about every 0.85s for 3.2s. |
| Water | Chill first (about 38% slower). A second chill, or ice, locks you still for a short freeze. |
| Glacial | Chill and a short freeze on the first hit. |
| Air | A very short stun. |
| Storm | A short shock stun. |
| Earth | A slightly longer stun. |
| Metal | A short stagger stun. |
| Venom | Poison. Stacks up to 3. Each stack is 1 damage per tick. |
| Blood | The same poison stacks (shown as bleed in the element list). |
| Light, Shadow, Arcane | Blind. Foes miss some attacks. You miss some swings, and the screen darkens. |

Icons and a countdown sit on the unit. Your own timers also sit on the side panel.

## Where to look

Open the built game with `?elementsPreview=1` to see a weak hit, a resisted hit, and every status at once.
